How to Play Sum Break

Everything you need to go from your first merge to clearing a 9×9 board.

The goal

Merge the circles on the board until only one is left.

Making a move

Choose two circles next to each other, above, below, left or right (not diagonal), that show the same number. Drag one onto the other, or tap the first and then the second. They become a single circle showing their total, in the space you dropped onto.

Empty spaces stay empty

After a merge, the space you dragged from stays empty for the rest of the level. Nothing slides in and no new circles appear. A circle left with no neighbours, or with only bigger numbers around it, can never be merged, and the level can’t be finished from there. That is what Undo is for.

The numbers

Numbers follow the sequence 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and so on, so two equal numbers always make the next one up. Numbers only ever grow, never shrink.

Wildcards

From level 30 you’ll meet wildcard circles. A wildcard merges with any number, in either direction, and applies its operation to it. Two wildcards can’t merge with each other.

WildcardWhat it doesExample
+1Adds 17 becomes 8
+2Adds 214 becomes 16
+3Adds 35 becomes 8
×2Doubles16 becomes 32
×3Triples1 becomes 3, then a +1 makes 4

A number outside the 1, 2, 4, 8 sequence always starts next to the wildcard that turns it back into one.

Strategies

  • Start small. The smallest number on the board can only merge with an equal neighbour or a wildcard, because everything else only gets bigger. Work out where the smallest numbers will go first.
  • Mind the edges. Circles in corners and along the sides have fewer neighbours, so they run out of partners first.
  • Look before you merge. On harder levels some matching pairs are decoys: merging them leaves another circle with no possible partner.
  • Keep the board in one piece. If the circles split into two groups that don’t touch, the groups can never meet again.
  • Undo freely. There’s no penalty for undoing, and trying a line of moves is often the quickest way to see a problem.

Hints

You get 3 hints per level. A hint makes the circle to move glow and draws an arrow showing which way to drag it. If your board can no longer be finished, the hint tells you how many moves to undo instead, without using one up. Solving a level gives you a fresh 3 the next time you play it.

Controls

  • Move: drag a circle onto its partner, or tap one and then the other.
  • Undo: the Undo button, or the Z or U key.
  • Hint: the Hint button, or the H key.
  • Restart: resets the board and the timer.
  • Levels: pick any of the 100 levels. Solved ones show a tick.
