About Sum Break

A free number puzzle for quick breaks and long thinks.

What we do

Sum Break is an independent browser puzzle game. We make one thing and try to make it well: a clean, fast number-merging puzzle that runs on any device, with no download, no account and no fuss.

Why we built it

We love puzzles that take seconds to learn and much longer to master. Sum Break takes the satisfying feeling of merging numbers and removes the luck: nothing slides, nothing random appears, and every board can be solved. Whether you have two minutes or twenty, there’s a level that fits.

How the levels are made

Every level is built backwards from a single number by splitting it apart, so each one is guaranteed to have a solution. A computer then plays hundreds of random games on each board to judge how hard it is, checks it for trap pairs, and orders the levels from easiest to hardest within each grid size.

What we care about

  • Free for everyone. All 100 levels, with no paywall.
  • Fair puzzles. Every board can be solved, with no guessing required.
  • Privacy. No sign-up and no tracking. Your progress stays in your browser.
  • Works everywhere. Phones, tablets and computers, in light or dark mode.
