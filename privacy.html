Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Sum Break is built to need as little of your data as possible. This policy explains what that means.

Information we collect

We don’t ask for your name, email address or any other personal details, and you don’t need an account to play.

Game progress stored on your device

Sum Break saves your progress using your browser’s local storage: solved levels, best times, hints used, unfinished boards and the level you last played. This information stays on your device. It isn’t sent to us and we can’t see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Sum Break doesn’t set cookies, doesn’t use analytics tools and doesn’t show adverts. If that changes, we’ll update this policy to explain what is collected and why.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, to keep the service secure and running. That is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

Sum Break is suitable for all ages. Because we don’t collect personal information, we don’t knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the UK GDPR and EU GDPR give you rights over your personal data, including the right to access or delete it. As we don’t hold personal data about players, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete, but you’re welcome to ask.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@playsumbreakgame.xyz.
