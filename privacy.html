Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Sum Break is built to need as little of your data as possible. This policy explains what we store, and what our advertising partners collect.

Information we collect

We don’t ask for your name, email address or any other personal details, and you don’t need an account to play.

Game progress stored on your device

Sum Break saves your progress using your browser’s local storage: solved levels, best times, hints used, unfinished boards and the level you last played. This information stays on your device. It isn’t sent to us and we can’t see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Advertising, cookies and analytics

Sum Break is free because it shows adverts. Ads and site analytics are provided by Ezoic and its advertising partners. To show, measure and personalise ads, they may use cookies and similar technologies and collect information such as your IP address, browser and device type, and how you use the site.

Where the law requires it, for example in the UK and EU, you’ll be asked for consent before non-essential cookies are used, through the consent pop-up that appears on your first visit. You can accept, reject or customise your choices, and you can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. How Ezoic and its partners use data is covered by their own privacy policies.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, to keep the service secure and running. That is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

Sum Break is a general-audience puzzle game and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the UK GDPR and EU GDPR give you rights over your personal data, including the right to access, delete or object to its use. We don’t hold personal data about players ourselves. For data used for advertising, you can change your consent choices at any time and contact Ezoic or its partners through their privacy policies. You’re also welcome to ask us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@playsumbreakgame.xyz.
