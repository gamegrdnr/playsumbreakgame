Where to start

The smallest number on this board is 2, and there are 2 of them. The smallest number can only ever merge with an equal neighbour or a wildcard, because everything else only gets bigger, so work out where those go first. The 2 in row 2, column 2 has only one possible partner, the 2 in row 3, column 2, so that pairing is forced.

Watch out for

There is 1 trap pair on this board: two equal numbers side by side where merging them either way leaves a circle that can never be finished. Before each merge, check that every neighbour of the two circles still has a partner afterwards.

Show the full solution (4 moves)
  1. Row 2, column 2: drag the 2 down onto the 2 to make 4
  2. Row 3, column 2: drag the 4 right onto the 4 to make 8
  3. Row 1, column 3: drag the 4 down onto the 4 to make 8
  4. Row 3, column 3: drag the 8 up onto the 8 to make 16
